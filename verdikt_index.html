Built for founders who'd rather decide than guess

Verdikt started as a real, personally-felt problem: founders burning hours scanning funding news with no reliable way to separate a genuine signal from noise. It became a real product once user testing — a founder, an investor, and a salesperson, three unrelated roles — independently converged on wanting the same thing: a clear judgment call, not more data to interpret themselves.

We believe founders shouldn't need a research team to make a confident call on a market signal. Information is abundant; judgment is scarce. Verdikt exists to make good judgment accessible to anyone building something new.

What we stand for

  • Judgment over data dumps — a verdict with reasoning beats a pile of matches
  • Transparency over confidence theater — we flag uncertainty instead of hiding it
  • Built for the time-constrained — every feature has to save a founder real minutes
  • Honest about limits — we document what the AI gets wrong, not just what it gets right
